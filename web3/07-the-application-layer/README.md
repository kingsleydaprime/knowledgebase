# The Application Layer

**What people actually build, and how it's actually gone.** This section is deliberately evaluative rather than promotional — most of these categories have a boom and a correction behind them, and the corrections are where the useful lessons are.

## Reading order
1. [[web3/07-the-application-layer/01-defi-primitives|defi-primitives]] — **[Advanced]** — AMMs and the constant-product invariant, over-collateralised lending, the three stablecoin designs and why one of them collapsed, perps — and **composability as both the feature and the systemic risk**
2. [[web3/07-the-application-layer/02-nfts-in-practice|nfts-in-practice]] — **[Intermediate]** — what the 2021 boom got wrong, what the registry genuinely provides, where it's working quietly, and the design decisions that determine whether a collection ages well
3. [[web3/07-the-application-layer/03-daos-and-governance|daos-and-governance]] — **[Intermediate]** — snapshot voting, timelocks, and why **token voting is plutocracy by construction**. Plus what a DAO is legally, which is less than most participants think
4. [[web3/07-the-application-layer/04-identity-and-naming|identity-and-naming]] — **[Intermediate]** — ENS as the clear success, **the Sybil problem underneath governance and lending**, and why every solution reintroduces a trusted party
5. [[web3/07-the-application-layer/05-gaming-and-the-rest|gaming-and-the-rest]] — **[Intermediate]** — play-to-earn's structural failure, social protocols, RWAs (where institutional money actually went), supply chain — and **the four-question test for any new proposal**
