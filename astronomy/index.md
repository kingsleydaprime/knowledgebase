# Astronomy and Space

## What astronomy is

**Astronomy is the science of everything beyond Earth: the Moon and planets, the Sun and other stars, galaxies, and the universe as a whole.**

Astronomers can't visit stars or run experiments on them. Almost everything we know comes from studying the light that reaches us. That light tells us what a star is made of, how hot it is, how fast it's moving, and how far away it is. And because light takes time to travel, looking at something far away means seeing it as it was long ago. **Looking further is looking back in time.**

This course needs no physics background and stands on its own. It also covers astrology honestly: what it is, how it differs from astronomy, what careful tests have found, and why it can feel so accurate.

## What you'll be able to do

By the end you will be able to:
- explain how the sky moves, why we have seasons, and find things in the night sky;
- explain how astronomers learn so much just from light;
- describe how stars are born, live and die, and where the elements in your body came from;
- explain how planets form, and what we've learned from planets around other stars;
- explain gravity from Newton to Einstein, including black holes, and why your phone's GPS needs relativity;
- describe how the universe began and how it's changing, including what's still unknown;
- explain how rockets and orbits work, and start doing real astronomy yourself.

## Key ideas to remember

1. **You can't experiment on the sky, only observe it.** Everything else follows. → [[astronomy/01-what-astronomy-is|01]]
2. **Seasons come from Earth's tilt, not its distance from the Sun.** Earth is actually closest to the Sun in January. → [[astronomy/02-the-sky-and-how-it-moves|02]]
3. **Helium was discovered in the Sun 27 years before it was found on Earth,** just from its light. → [[astronomy/03-light-and-instruments|03]]
4. **Almost every element heavier than lithium was made in stars.** You're partly made of star material. → [[astronomy/04-stars|04]]
5. **Without Einstein's relativity, GPS would drift about 10 km a day.** → [[astronomy/06-gravity-and-relativity|06]]
6. **Space itself is expanding, so the universe has no centre.** → [[astronomy/07-cosmology|07]]
7. **About 95% of the universe is made of things we haven't identified.** → [[astronomy/07-cosmology|07]]
8. **To catch something ahead of you in orbit, you slow down.** → [[astronomy/08-spaceflight-and-exploration|08]]
9. **Astrology does no better than chance in fair tests,** yet feels personal because of general statements we read ourselves into. → [[astronomy/09-astrology-honestly|09]]
