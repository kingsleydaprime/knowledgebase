## Why this round matters more than people expect

The database is where most real performance problems live and where most real data-loss bugs originate. In a backend interview, **the database questions are where they find out whether you've operated a system or only queried one.**

The tell: whether you reach for `EXPLAIN` before you reach for an opinion.
