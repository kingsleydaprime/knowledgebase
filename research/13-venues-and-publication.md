# Venues & the Publication Process

**[reference]** · **⏳ outline — deep note in progress** (part of the research-course build). Opens Part C (getting it out).

Where research *goes* — and how the sausage is made. Choosing the right venue and understanding the publication pipeline, which differs sharply by field.

## Will cover
- **Preprints (arXiv)** — post first, get feedback and priority, cite before peer review; norms and cautions; why CS/ML/physics live on arXiv.
- **Conferences vs journals** — the big divide: **CS/ML is conference-first** (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, ICRA for robotics, ISCA/DAC for chips — competitive, fast, prestigious), most **other engineering/sciences are journal-first** (slower, longer, revise-and-resubmit). Which your field uses changes everything.
- **Choosing a venue** — fit (is your topic in scope?), tier/prestige vs acceptance odds, timeline, page limits, open-access; reading the [[research/11-paper-types-and-writing-styles|target venue's]] recent papers to calibrate.
- **Venue quality signals & traps** — acceptance rates, h5-index; **predatory venues/journals** to avoid (pay-to-publish scams), how to spot them.
- **The submission pipeline** — abstract/paper deadlines, formatting/templates, anonymization for double-blind, supplementary material, camera-ready.
- **Metrics (and their misuse)** — citations, h-index, impact factor — useful signals, dangerous targets (Goodhart's law).
- **Open access & licensing** — green/gold OA, preprint + published version, who owns your work.
