# Governance, Risk and Compliance

**This section is the management side of security: deciding what to protect and how much to spend, running security as a program across an organisation of people, and proving you meet the obligations the law and your customers impose.**

The rest of the course builds controls; this section decides *which* controls are worth building, *who* owns them, and *how you prove* they work. It's where security stops being only technical and becomes a business function — and where the senior and leadership roles live. The hands-on work here is the paperwork that actually runs security: a risk register, a policy set, a security metric, and a software bill of materials.

## What you'll be able to do

By the end of this section you will be able to:

- score and rank risks in a risk register, and choose a defensible treatment for each;
- place the security career paths and the certifications that fit them;
- write a policy → standard → procedure hierarchy and compute a security metric for it;
- generate and scan an SBOM, and answer "are we affected by this vulnerability?" in seconds.
