# 08 — Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

The management and organizational side of security — deciding *what* to protect, *how much* to invest, and *proving* you meet obligations. The part that makes security a business function, not just a technical one, and where senior/leadership roles live. Part of the [[cybersecurity/README|Cybersecurity course]].

## Reading order

1. [[cybersecurity/08-governance-risk-and-compliance/01-risk-and-frameworks|Risk & Frameworks]] — **[Intermediate]** — risk (likelihood × impact) and how to assess/treat it, plus the standard frameworks (NIST CSF/RMF, ISO 27001, CIS Controls) and compliance regimes (GDPR, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, SOC 2)
2. [[cybersecurity/08-governance-risk-and-compliance/02-certifications-and-career|Certifications & Career]] — **[Beginner]** — the cert landscape (Security+, CEH, OSCP, CISSP, and friends) and how the security career paths map to this course
3. [[cybersecurity/08-governance-risk-and-compliance/03-governance-and-the-human-layer|Governance & the Human Layer]] — **[Intermediate]** — **the "G": running security as a program** (policy→standard→procedure, roles, the CISO), and why the biggest vulnerability is people — security awareness and a **blameless reporting culture**
4. [[cybersecurity/08-governance-risk-and-compliance/04-third-party-and-supply-chain-risk|Third-Party & Supply-Chain Risk]] — **[Intermediate → Advanced]** — **the risk you inherit from dependencies and vendors** (SolarWinds, Log4Shell, Target), SBOMs, vendor vetting, and why "you're only as secure as your least-secure dependency"
