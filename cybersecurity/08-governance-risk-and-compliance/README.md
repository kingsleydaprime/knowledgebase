# 08 — Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

The management and organizational side of security — deciding *what* to protect, *how much* to invest, and *proving* you meet obligations. The part that makes security a business function, not just a technical one, and where senior/leadership roles live. Part of the [[cybersecurity/README|Cybersecurity course]].
