## Note on framing

Security interviews reward **threat modelling out loud**: who's the attacker, what do they want, what's the trust boundary, what happens when it's crossed. Reciting the OWASP Top 10 is table stakes; reasoning about a specific system's exposure is the signal.

Also: for anything offensive, **always frame it within authorisation** — scope, rules of engagement, and a written agreement. Candidates who talk about attacking things without mentioning authorisation raise a flag, regardless of technical skill. → [[cybersecurity/02-ethical-hacking/01-rules-of-engagement-and-legal|rules of engagement]]
