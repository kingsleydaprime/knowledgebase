## Scope note

`frontend/frameworks/react` and `02-next` are still scaffold in this vault — the written depth is in the **animation** courses ([[frontend/frameworks/gsap/README|GSAP]], [[frontend/frameworks/framer-motion/README|Framer Motion]], [[frontend/frameworks/threejs/README|Three.js]]), which is unusual and worth leaning into. Most frontend candidates cannot talk about the compositor, `requestAnimationFrame`, or why `transform` beats `top` — you can, and it's a genuine differentiator in a role with any motion or 3D work.
