## What these interviews actually test

For **AI engineering** roles the differentiator is almost never model knowledge — it's whether you treat an LLM system as **software with a probabilistic component** or as a demo. The questions that separate people are about **evaluation, failure modes, and cost**, not about prompt tricks.

If you can say "here's my eval set, here's the success rate, here's what changed when I swapped models," you're ahead of most candidates.
