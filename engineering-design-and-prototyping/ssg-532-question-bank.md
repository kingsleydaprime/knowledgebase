# SSG 532 Question Bank

Every question from the SSG 532 (Engineering Design and Prototyping) exam paper, 2025/2026 first semester, in one place. The questions are left unsolved on purpose, and each one points to the lesson that teaches it.

**How to use this:** attempt each question closed-book, on paper, as if in the exam. Then open the linked lesson and compare. The value is in the attempt.

**Instructions on the paper:** attempt any four questions. Time allowed: 2 hours. Credit is given for orderly presentation.

---

## Question 1

**(a)** Effective problem definition is fundamental to successful engineering design. Analyse four (4) established methods used for problem definition. *(6 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/04-problem-definition|04 — Problem Definition]]

**(b)** Illustrate the SCAMPER technique as a creative problem-solving tool in engineering design. *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/09-concept-generation-2|09 — Concept Generation 2: SCAMPER]]

## Question 3

## Question 5

**(a)** Design a comprehensive testing protocol to evaluate its performance and usability. The protocol should detail the specific tests to be conducted, data collected and the success criteria used. *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/25-prototyping-and-testing-protocols|25 — Prototyping and Testing Protocols]]

> The question says "its performance and usability" but doesn't name a product. Choose one (the study desk stand works) and say so at the start of your answer.

**(b)** Justify the purpose and application of Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) in the product development cycle. Using a simple product, such as a retractable ballpoint pen, provide two (2) examples of potential failure modes, their effects and the actions a design team might take to mitigate them. *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/27-failure-mode-and-effects-analysis|27 — Failure Mode and Effects Analysis]]

**(c)** Support why detailed and accurate engineering drawing is still a vital skill in the age of 3D CAD and rapid prototyping. What critical information do these drawings convey that a 3D model file alone may not explicitly communicate for manufacturing? *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/21-engineering-drawings-and-dfma|21 — Engineering Drawings and DFMA]]

## Question 6

**(a)** Compare and contrast the properties and typical applications of thermoplastics and thermosetting polymers in product design. Provide two (2) examples of products for each material type. *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/22-materials-polymers-and-frames|22 — Materials: Polymers and Frames]]

**(b)** Evaluate the four (4) key principles of Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA). For each principle, describe how its application influences the final design of a product. *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/21-engineering-drawings-and-dfma|21 — Engineering Drawings and DFMA]]

**(c)** In designing a lightweight, high-strength bicycle frame, recommend a suitable material and a corresponding manufacturing process for this application. Justify your choices in detail, considering factors such as material properties, production volume and cost. *(5 marks)* → [[engineering-design-and-prototyping/22-materials-polymers-and-frames|22 — Materials: Polymers and Frames]]
