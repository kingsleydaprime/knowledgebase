## Here is the comprehensive, unified Senior Secondary School (SS1 to SS3) Mathematics curriculum based on the Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC) guidelines.

## 📚 Senior Secondary School Year 1 (SS1)

Focuses on strengthening foundational arithmetic, core algebra, and basic deductive geometry.

## First Term

- Number Bases: Conversion of bases, basic arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication) in binary and other bases.
- Modular Arithmetic: Core principles of modular arithmetic, operations, and application to real-life cycles (clocks and calendars).
- Indices & Logarithms: Laws of indices, standard form, converting indices to logs, and calculations using logarithm/antilogarithm tables (powers and roots).
- Sets: Set notation, operations (union, intersection, complement), and Venn diagrams (solving problems up to 3 sets).

## Second Term

- Logical Reasoning: Antecedents, consequents, conditional statements, connectives (and, or, if-then, implies), and truth tables.
- Linear Equations & Formulas: Solving linear equations, simultaneous linear equations, and changing the subject of a formula.
- Variations: Solving problems on direct, inverse, joint, and partial variations.
- Deductive Geometry & Constructions: Angles on a straight line, parallel lines, properties of triangles, and constructing angles ($30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$).

## Third Term

- Quadratic Equations: Solving by factorization and completing the square methods.
- Trigonometry: Trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, tangent), graphs of trig functions, and application to angles of elevation and depression.
- Mensuration: Perimeter and areas of plane shapes; surface areas and volumes of solid shapes (cylinders, cones, spheres, pyramids).
- Statistics: Presentation of data (frequency tables, bar charts, histograms, pie charts), and calculation of mean, median, and mode for ungrouped data.

---

## 📚 Senior Secondary School Year 2 (SS2)

Introduces advanced algebra, coordinate geometry, circle theorems, and deeper statistical metrics.

## First Term

- Logarithms: Further operations, logarithms of numbers less than 1, and characteristics/mantissa rules.
- Approximations & Percentage Error: Rounding off numbers, significant figures, and calculating percentage error.
- Sequence and Series: Core principles of Arithmetic Progression (AP) and Geometric Progression (GP), finding the $n$-th term and sum of terms.
- Quadratic Equations (Advanced): Solving via the quadratic formula (almighty formula), properties of roots, and sketching quadratic graphs.

## Second Term

- Simultaneous Linear and Quadratic Equations: Solving a system of two equations where one is linear and the other is quadratic.
- Linear Inequalities: Solving inequalities in one and two variables, and graphical representation of solutions.
- Algebraic Fractions: Simplification of fractions, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and substitution.
- Circle Geometry (Theorems): Core theorems regarding chords, angles subtended at the center/circumference, and cyclic quadrilaterals.

## Third Term

- Tangents to a Circle: Theorems concerning tangents from an external point, angles in alternate segments, and constructions.
- Trigonometry (Further): Sine Rule and Cosine Rule for non-right-angled triangles, and solving bearings and distances.
- Coordinate Geometry: Midpoint of a line, gradient of a straight line, distance between two points, and equations of a straight line.
- Statistics & Probability: Cumulative frequency curves (ogives), measures of dispersion (range, variance, standard deviation), and basic probability.

---

## 📚 Senior Secondary School Year 3 (SS3)

Concentrates on revision, exam-critical topics (WAEC/NECO/JAMB prep), and advanced mathematical applications.

## First Term

- Matrices & Determinants: Introduction to matrices, addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, and finding determinants/inverses of $2 \times 2$ matrices.
- Vectors in a Plane: Components of vectors, magnitude, direction, addition/subtraction, and scalar products.
- Integrals & Derivatives (Intro to Calculus): Concepts of differentiation (rates of change) and integration (area under a curve) for basic polynomials.
- Surds: Rationalization of denominators and basic operations with irrational numbers.
- Latitude and Longitude: Calculating distances along great circles (meridians and equator) and small circles (parallels of latitude).

## Second Term & Third Term (Revision & Exams)

- Further Probability: Mutually exclusive events, independent events, and tree diagrams.
- Comprehensive WAEC/NECO Exam Practice: Intensive revision of structural essays and multiple-choice sections based on past question papers.
- JAMB/UTME Prep: Focused timed testing spanning all concepts from SS1 through SS3.
